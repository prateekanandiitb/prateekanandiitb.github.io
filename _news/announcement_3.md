Awarded the prestigious **ANRF Early Career Research Grant (ECRG)** for performing research in drop microfluidics. The research will aim at characterizing the migration of drops in channel flows as a function of their deformability and size. The nature of this work will be both analytical and numerical. I have an open TAP (Teaching Assistantship through Project) position for prospective PhD students for this project.
