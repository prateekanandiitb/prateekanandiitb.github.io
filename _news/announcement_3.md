Our group has been awarded the prestigious **ANRF Early Career Research Grant (ECRG)** to support research in drop microfluidics. The project will investigate the migration of deformable drops in channel flows as a function of drop size and deformability through a combination of analytical and numerical approaches.

A fully funded **TAP (Teaching Assistantship through Project)** PhD position is currently available in this project.
